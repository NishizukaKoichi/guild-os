# Guild OS v1 Capability Index

Updated: 2026-08-24

Status: Historical compatibility index, not a completion authority.

The product grew beyond the original v1 capability checklist. The authoritative requirements are
the [product specification](product-specification.md), the executable release contract is
[full-spec acceptance](full-spec-acceptance.md), and current evidence is tracked only in the
[product completion matrix](product-completion-matrix.md).

The following capability groups are implemented in Core and remain mandatory regression coverage:

- Human Root sovereignty, Constitution, two-party transfer, Break Glass, and Chronicle;
- Human, Agent, Service, and Guild Actor membership through Role, Capability, and Space boundaries;
- Context Graph, broad Memory, governed Knowledge, semantic retrieval, and exact-version citations;
- recursive Activity plus compatible Goal, Project, Quest, and Step workflows;
- Decisions, Announcements, Inbox, Conversations, onboarding, offboarding, and Contribution;
- Ask, inspectable Plan, policy-gated Act, Agent limits, approval, Kill, and idempotency;
- purchaser-owned Connections, Automation, Federation, export, retention, backup, and restore;
- Personal + AI, fixed Profiles, Purpose-first Other, reusable Blueprint, and Space override;
- English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, keyboard, accessibility, desktop, tablet, and mobile UX.

These capabilities do not by themselves make Guild OS complete. The product also includes the
separate Owned Distribution and requires exact signed releases, purchaser-owned Installer and
Updater evidence, independent restore, hosted CI, operational signing custody, and professional
sale review. Synthetic clean-room success, Core completion, local commit, push, or CI green is only
one milestone.

Use the matrix for every completion claim. If any authoritative row is not `Implemented and
verified`, report **Guild OSは未完成です。**
